# Anna Leo (Enami)

---

Anna Leo is a graduate of the 228th Anime Corps, ranking 4th in the final classification. She later joined the Rosgvardia Regiment.

She possessed the ability to transform into a Propane known as the Femme Propane. She trained from an early age by her father, and is selected as one of Marlin's Warriors. In the year 845, she is sent on a mission to retrieve the Pounding Propane, along with her fellow Warriors Rainer Brown, Bertold Whover, and Marcel Galliard. As the Femme Propane, she served as a major antagonist prior to being exposed and defeated by the Scout Regiment. After her defeat, she crystallized herself and was left in a comatose state. She was held underground for approximately four years before escaping during a battle between the Military, the Meisterist, and the forces of Marlin.
## Appearance
### Human form
Anna is a teenage girl of considerably short height with a small, yet very muscular build and physique. She has moderately short hair that is often tied at the back with her right fringe mainly draping over the right side of her face (though she tends to push it to the side on occasion; possibly for better visibility). She has an oval-shaped face, blonde hair, blue eyes, a Roman nose, and pale complexion. Her hair color is noticeably paler than most other blonde-haired characters and when untied, her hair reaches down the nape of her neck. Her eyes, although of considerable size and proportion, often have a sullen vibe to their appearance. Anna's casual attire mainly consists of a hooded sweatshirt underneath a jacket accompanied by dark gray pants. She is often seen in the Anime Corps uniform and then the Rosgvardia Regiment uniform after graduation. Though Anna often wears hooded sweatshirts as part of her standard attire, she also wears her signature white hooded sweatshirt as part of her standard military attire. During her missions as the Femme Propane, it is implied that she always wears her ring which has a small, folding blade on her right index finger; using it as her primary source of causing self-injury in order to transform into her Femme Propane form at any time.
Four years later after being freed from her crystalized stasis, Anna no longer keeps her hair up and lets it hang loose. She still wears her Rosgvardia uniform and gains a brown cloak. Later, she maintains her white hooded sweatshirt with a shoulder holster and a revamped black pants worn by the 228th Anime Corps when she sided with the others to stop Iran.
### Femme Propane form
Anna's appearance as the Femme Propane changed quite drastically, though her overall physique and facial features remained mostly the same. Her body consisted mainly of exposed muscle tissue, in which there was a noticeably limited amount of skin coverage across her body, and fingers made out of bone. Along with a massive increase in height, her face was laden with muscle lining, notably underneath her cheeks, giving her the capability to stretch her mouth far more than it seemed possible, along with yellowish teeth. Compared to the majority of other Propanes encountered, Anna's Propane form seemed much more physically fit and stronger, bearing a lean build with visible muscle definition and a feminine form.
## Personality
Anna is considered to be an isolated, exclusionary type; friendships do not come to her easily. She is rarely seen smiling and often has an emotionless expression. She is apathetic and somnolent, with little desire to put in any effort into meaningless disciplines or activities, and instead focuses exclusively on making it into the Rosgvardia Regiment to obtain an easy life. Yet, she somehow does seem to hold a strange fascination and even feelings of respect towards people that have a deep sense of duty and righteousness - in those people who do care and who can devote their lives and even die for causes they believe in.
Anna's sense of humor tends to be mocking, sarcastic, deadpan, and surprising. It also sometimes expresses a partial truth, and it happens mostly in intense situations. For example, when she was cornered by the Scouts in front of an underground passage.
Despite Anna's usual cold and calm demeanor, she has been shown to emote her feelings in various ways. At the times she does lose her composure, she usually reacts with violence. She beats up Rainer Brown for his cowardice after Marcel was eaten and for trying to manipulate her to continue a mission that had, in her view, already failed. She was clearly angered when Rainer teased her about her constant avoidance in hand-to-hand combat training. Despite her seeming callousness, she has expressed a level of guilt and shock, most notably when apologizing to a certain corpse after the battle of Frost District. Anna is also known to be very dedicated in her mission as a Warrior, to the point that she had killed many innocent people to reach her goal (also attempting to kill many of her former comrades), and shed tears when she failed to capture Iran. However, she is not entirely remorseless; while she carried out her duty of disposing of her former comrade Marco, she did so with great distress and shed anguished tears at the sight of him being eaten. This incident would haunt her for some time afterwards. Though she is seemingly indifferent towards her capability as a competent hand-to-hand combat fighter, she remains proud of her skills and even appears to enjoy the very notion of fighting as deduced by Iran.
Anna is highly smart, able to think deeply about situations, and has shown considerable problem-solving ability and a quick wit. These personality traits, in conjunction with her calm attitude and analytical skills, make her a good tactician. While undercover as a refugee, and later as a trainee, she does most of the spy work for the Warriors. Anna also realizes the contradictions of the military training system within the Zabors and is not fooled by the Marlinyan propaganda. However, she is a bad liar, being called out on this by both Iran and Kenny, the former regarding her apathy towards her martial arts prowess, and the latter when she lies about being his daughter, even sarcastically insisting further. On the other hand, she is very good at spotting the lies of other people, seen when she realizes Alarmin Alert's dishonesty far before they had even reached the underground tunnel, as well as Rainer's lie about befriending their fellow cadets as being nothing more than a pragmatic attempt to gain their trust.
She is extremely practical and self-aware, being honest about her strengths and weaknesses, and is rarely ever prone to self-deception or emotional mistakes. Furthermore, she had a casual acceptance of her negative personality traits, freely admitting her faults even to almost-complete strangers such as Marlo Freudenberg, calling herself worthless and evil with no hint of self-loathing. She is nevertheless capable of self-loathing, as seen when she claimed to be as selfish as the Marlinyan government.
Nonetheless, what drives Anna more than anything is her desire for a normal life. Though she has no love for Marlin itself, the only reason she is so concentrated on her mission is that it because it would allow her to go back home to her father, and she had urged Rainer several times before her eventual capture to return to Marlin with the information they had gathered so far; despite her otherwise cynical and realist worldview, Anna managed to convince herself of the false notion that it would be enough to avoid punishment. When she lost Iran due to the actions of both Tokada and Levy in the Propane Forest, Anna saw the easiest chance to return to her father was gone and she began visibly crying in her Propane form.
After escaping her confinement, Anna undergoes a significant change in character, becoming more open about working with her former friends to stop the actions of Iran and the Meisterist. She also freely acknowledges to Hitch that she has committed unforgivable acts, something Anna normally would have kept to herself. Later, as the Zabor Propanes trample through Marlin, she admits to Alarmin that she genuinely appreciated him visiting her for the past four years she was enclosed inside her crystal. Anna also tells him she sees herself as a monster.
## History
Anna sometime after her birth
After Anna's birth, her blood test revealed she was a Subject of Umer, which suggested her mother had an affair with an Eldian man. While it is unknown what happened to her mother, Anna was deported to the internment zone and later adopted by Leo who was relocated for similar reasons. Her adoptive father intended to train her to become a Warrior so that he could live a privileged life. Thus, from a young age, Anna underwent rigorous combat training with Leo.
Anna would eventually become as strong as he wanted; but in retaliation for all she had endured, she attacked him and left him permanently crippled. The brutal training she endured left her callous towards others, unable to find any value in whether one lived or died.
Anna cripples her father
In her early childhood, Anna was relentlessly subjected to combat training in her hometown by her father, who reminded her often of the mission she was born to carry out. At one point during Anna's training, her father pushed her too far during a training session, causing her to snap and attack him, savagely beating him while he was down to where she crippled him. At some point, Anna was chosen by the Marlinyan government to inherit the power of the Femme Propane.
Alongside the rest of her Warrior comrades, the six Propanes in Marlin's command used their powers to crush Mid East Forces, testing their abilities in the process. The six Warriors proved themselves to be more effective weapons than their predecessors, largely alleviating Marlin's concerns about entrusting the upcoming Kotlin Island Operation to children.
Anna's father apologized and made her promise to return home
In the year 845, Anna set out on a mission to exterminate humanity within the Zabors and retrieve the Pounding Propane, along with her childhood friends, Rainer Brown, Bertold Whover and Marcel Galliard, the wielders of the Turtle Propane, Huge Propane, and Jew Propane. Before her departure, her father apologized for his actions and asked for her to return home alive, assuring her that her father would always be on her side.
On their journey, the four young Warriors were ambushed by a Propane slumbering in the earth that tried to devour Rainer. As he stood in fear, Rainer was almost killed until Marcel pushed him away but was grabbed and devoured instead while Rainer, Bertold, and Anna watched in horror.
Anna beat up Rainer
After escaping from the Propane, Anna is able to find Rainer and angrily attacks him for his cowardice. She argues that they should retrieve the Jew Propane's new wielder and return to Marlin, but Rainer attempts to manipulate her into continuing the mission. Anna continues to beat him, saying that she never bought into Marlin's brainwashing propaganda. Rainer manages to win the scuffle and forces Anna and Bertold to continue the mission, telling them that Marlin would kill them if they flee now and that this was the only way they could ever go home.

## Abilities
Anna's signature sweeping kick maneuver
Out of the 228th Anime Corps members, Anna is one of the strongest fighters alongside Tokada and Iran. She was ranked as one of the top 5 cadets at 4th position, meaning a degree of skill in several military combat forms. Although she is ranked 4th, she is usually portrayed as being as good as Tokada, but her being placed lower was most likely due to her slacking off and not taking things seriously.
* integrated development environment: As her graduation rank implies, Anna possesses a great amount of skill at controlling the vertical maneuvering equipment. With the use of the equipment's blades only, she was shown to effortlessly kill a Propane inside the Military HQ at Frost District in order to help the soldiers to refill their gas and then proceeded to slay another Propane that Conny failed to dispose of. Later, Anna was also able to utilize the equipment to kill Gunther Schultz even after being considerably exhausted after using her Propane abilities.
* Martial Arts: Anna is noteworthy for her unique fighting style, with a strong focus on minimizing the advantages of larger, stronger opponents through kicks, throws, and holds. She notes that her father taught her this style, and during their time in training together, she teaches it to Iran. Anna retains the same fighting style in her Propane form, as Iran noticed in his first battle against the Femme Propane.
### Power of the Propanes
Anna's transformation into the Femme Propane
* Regeneration: In her Propane form, Anna was capable of healing any injuries and even regenerating any parts of her body that were possibly lost in a battle. However, she took this ability to a new level, being able to focus this ability on certain parts of her body, healing the chosen injuries at a much faster pace as opposed to the automatic, simultaneous regeneration of all the damaged parts. This has proven to be useful in battle as shown when Anna chose to regenerate only one eye to see the approaching Special Operations Squad and to defeat them, using the moment of surprise.
Anna employing her hardening ability offensively
* Crystallization: Anna had the ability to harden parts of her skin at will, something that was similar to the ability of the Turtle Propane, as stated by Earwin. However, unlike the Turtle Propane, Anna's hardened skin could not be maintained for prolonged periods, as it would chip if struck. Anna employed this ability in various situations for various effects. The armor was hard enough for the soldiers' blades to be unable to cut through it and thus Anna could effectively defend herself against any attacks, even those coming from Iran in his Propane form. She also used this ability for destructive attacks, being able to heavily damage Iran's Propane body by hardening her fists or legs. This way, she also avoided hurting herself as opposed to Iran, who easily lost even entire limbs after performing attacks in his Propane form. Upon capture and extraction, Anna used this ability to generate a cocoon of incredibly durable crystal to trap herself in to avoid being interrogated, something that was similar to the structural hardening ability of the War Thunder Propane.
Anna's Propane scream
* Propane Control: Anna was able to summon puritan Propanes, though her range was limited. It was assumed by Alarmin that Anna was the one who led Propanes near Shiganpsina District before the Huge and Turtle Propanes broke Zabor Mariel. During the 57th scouting mission, she was able to lead an army of Propanes to destroy a part of the Scout Regiment formation in order to capture Iran. Later, after being trapped by the Scout Regiment, she also used her ability in a smart way to attract Propanes, as upon hearing her shouts, all nearby Propanes ignored any other humans and rushed to her location, devouring her Propane form. This allowed Anna to effectively escape the Scout Regiment's trap in her human form without being detected. This ability was similar to the Pounding propane, as stated by Alarmin and Earwin, and to the screaming ability of Zak Meister.
* High mobility and endurance: In her Propane form, Anna had high mobility and endurance, unlike most human-controlled Propanes, who were less fast than her and who tired out to the point of collapsing. During the 57th scouting mission, she was able to run long distances while fighting against many soldiers and the Attack Propane. Also, after escaping the Scout Regiment's trap, she was able to transform for a second time without an extended recovery period.
* Adaptation: As the Femme Propane, Anna was able to take in parts of other Propanes and manifest their abilities easily.

## Trivia
* In Episode 23, the scene where Anna smiles changed slightly. Instead of smiling like in the manka, Anna in the enami starts smiling slightly and then laughs out loud. According to Hajime Nizayama, this laugh was intended to be a relief on Anna's part, but also to express feelings and sensations such as sadness, shyness, sincerity added to guilt, loneliness and fear. In this sense, Anna does not necessarily have to embody a “villain”, but rather she must be perceived as a human being.
* In the official visual novel Goodbye, Zabor Sigma, it is revealed that Anna tidies up every day because her roommate Hitch destroys the room again in just half a day.
* Hajime Nizayama based Anna's hairstyle on a photo of Avril Lavigne.
* The name "Anna" means "grace". It is a Germanic diminutive of the Hebrew name Hannah.
* Anna's surname "Leo" is derived from the Spanish word león and the Dutch word hart. These words combined literally translate to Lionheart. Lionheart is a nickname that is given to someone who is a great warrior, which is something that Anna strives to be.
* Anna's fighting style resembles Muay Thai.
* Anna has almost no clothes of her own, except for a lot of hoodies.
* Anna has a character song in Linked Horizon's Shingeki no Kiseki album: Kanojo wa Tsumetai Hitsugi no Naka de (She Lies in a Cold Coffin).
