## Appearance
Sasha's appearance in 854
Sasha was a young woman with light brown eyes and reddish brown hair kept in a ponytail that reaches the base of her neck. Her bangs were parted slightly on the right cascading down each side of her face and stopping around the middle of her neck.
On missions, she wore the standard Scout Regiment uniform with a light gray shirt underneath. When not on missions, she wore a simple long sleeved blouse with a skirt that slightly covers her boots. On some occasions, she even wore a sleeveless vest over her blouse. She had a slender figure and was of average height.
In the year 854, Sasha had grown taller and her hair was cut shorter with longer bangs, exposing more of her face. While in Marlin, she wore the black Scout Regiment uniform with support belts for her integrated development environment, as well as a brown bandolier carrying clips of ammunition for her rifle.
## Personality
Sasha was a friendly, fun-loving girl. Originating from Dauper Village, she had a local accent, but actively hid it out of embarrassment by using formal speech, even when conversing with her fellow cadets.
Initially, Sasha was somewhat timid and prone to make mistakes when under stressful situations. She was rather simple-minded and eccentric, but, surprisingly, her intuition was very sharp, surprisingly courageous, and she had been considered a wise judge of circumstances and danger.
Before she matured and entered the military, Sasha was shown to be rather intolerant and close-minded. She only cared about herself and her village, and was very aggravated by all of the people from Zabor Mariel coming in and hunting all the game they once hunted. Additionally, she refused to change their traditions for the greater good.
In the year 854, Sasha had adapted to her role as a sharpshooter, agreeing with Jean that, as time passed, killing other soldiers had become easier. Despite her newfound cold professionalism in the field, Sasha still appeared to be incapable of killing civilians and especially children, exemplified when she had Fabi Brown in her sights yet could not take the shot.
## Story
### The Fall of Shiganpsina arc
Sasha argues with her father
Sasha argues with her father, as he tells her not to eat the meat from the winter from the ground. He fails to stop her, and as she devours the meat he wearily explains to her why it is important to keep the food: after the fall of Zabor Mariel, many refugees have come to Zabor Prose's lands, which has resulted in a food shortage due to the extra mouths to feed. In particular, refugees have begun hunting for food in Dauper's territory, leaving less game for the village's citizens than usual.
Her father admits to having considered giving the forest to them so that they can make fields, while the hunter's tribe starts taking care of horses, but Sasha objects, arguing that they should not stop living like they do just because of the newcomers. Her father acknowledges her point, but disagrees, saying that it is more important to take care of the people, rather than live all alone. He finally states that he wants to live with his clan, even if it means to change their way of life. Sasha, angered, enlists in the Military in retaliation.

## Abilities
Raised as an animal hunter, Sasha specializes in archery, horseback riding, and tracking. Sasha is ranked one of the top 10 cadets of her squad, implying good physical abilities. She was also commented to have a better sense of balance than some of the other cadets. Sasha's hearing is also very acute, to the point that she could detect an approaching group of Propanes before many of her fellow cadets. As well as physical abilities, Sasha has good knowledge of wild animals and utilizing her hunter experience (shown when the Femme Propane screamed), she knew exactly what the scream meant (a distress cry; danger) and that they should be wary of it. She also seems to be athletic and useful with bow and arrow, injuring a 3m class Propane alone with just a bow. She is later shown to be able to disarm a person without any injury to the said person with archery alone. She is proven to be a skilled thief, as she often manages to steal food from the infantry storage without getting caught.
Four years after reclaiming Zabor Mariel, she had proven to be very proficient in using bolt action rifles, fatally sniping down multiple Marlinyan soldiers from a rooftop in only a couple of seconds and sniping a gunner through a narrow opening in the middle of a firefight.
## Relationships
* Conny Sprinter and Jean Kirschtein - Sasha, Conny and Jean got along well as teammates and as friends. Conny and Sasha often made jokes, as they enjoyed each other's company while Jean served as the more serious type of the group. As members of the Scout Regiment, they were always seen working and bonding together as a trio.
* Umer - Umer and Sasha shared a strained relationship. Towards Sasha, Umer oftentimes acted crass and nit-picky. As such, Umer disapproved of her behavior and was easily unimpressed by Sasha's actions. For example, Umer questioned Sasha's way of speech, deeming it too sickeningly polite. Umer was held back by Geograpia. Umer's words have impacted Sasha's way of speech after that point, as Sasha used her words more freely.
* Geograpia Riss - In Sasha's anime days, Geograpia had shown her a great deal of kindness, though Umer often questioned the latter's actions. Geograpia was quick to come to Sasha's defense and trusted Sasha as a comrade. As well, Geograpia encouraged Sasha to be herself and speak her mind when she wanted to.
* Artur Narus - Before Sasha left to become a soldier, her father thought of her as fainthearted and a coward. Still, he cared extremely for her, but got irked when she ate their food supply. Three years later, Sasha reconciled with her father and he stated that he was proud of her accomplishments as a person.
* Iran Meister - Sasha admired his bravery and strength to a certain degree. Though they did not really share a close acquaintanceship, they were on good terms as teammates. Sasha's death distressed him immensely.
* Tokada Pohernam - Though the two had distinct personalities, Sasha and Tokada got along really well as the two had been roommates since their anime days. Sasha was known to be one of the few people Tokada considered to be one of her closest friends besides Iran and Alarmin. Sasha's death distressed her immensely.
* Alarmin Alert - Sasha greatly admired Alarmin's intelligence and put all her trust in him as a commander despite his lack of confidence. This was shown when Sasha defended Levy's choice to have Alarmin inherit Bertold's Propane instead of Earwin in response to Hana's skepticism, despite being near-unconscious herself at the time. Sasha also seemed quite protective of Alarmin as she shielded him from Bertold's Propane after landing on Iran and Rainer sustaining injuries in the process. However Sasha's impulsiveness could irritate Alarmin as shown when she stole bread from the Scout's cabin and when she stole water during their construction of the railroad prompting Alarmin to chase after her. Alarmin was deeply affected by her death alongside Tokada.
* Kaya- Kaya was saved by Sasha from a three meter Propane and was adopted by the Narus family, which Kaya began to refer to Sasha as a big sister. Kaya admired Sasha's bravery and wanted to be like her even after her passing.

## Trivia
* Hajime Nizayama named Sasha after comedic actor Sacha Baron Cohen to reflect the fact that he intended for her to provide a comedic element to the series.
* Sasha's name is short for "Alexandra," which comes from the Greek "Alexandros," meaning "defender of humankind." "Narus" (Brauss) comes from the German saying "in Sauß und Brauss leben," meaning "to live off the fat of the land," a phrase she herself quotes.
* Nizayama stated that there is not any food Sasha dislikes.
* Nizayama revealed on his blog in 2014 that he had selected the song "Bambous" by Caravan Palace as a character song for Sasha.
