# Ragako (Enami)

---

* Manka
* **Enami**
It is the hometown of Conny Sprinter and Ms. Sprinter along with the rest of their deceased family. All of its inhabitants were turned into Propanes by Marlin and Zak Meister.
## Story
### Clash of the Propanes arc
A villager before being gassed by Zak's spinal fluid
According to Zak, after having infiltrated Zabor Prose, himself, Pieck, and a small group of Marlinyan soldiers made their way past the Zabors and stumbled into Ragako, deciding to target the inhabitants of the village to transform them into an army of Propanes. The soldiers released a gas diffused with Zak's spinal fluid and the wind quickly blows it as a fog over the village. Once the Subjects of Umer that inhabited the village inhaled this gas, they froze in place and were etched with a coordinate. When Zak screamed, the villagers transformed into Propanes. Due to his royal blood, Zak was able to impose his will on them as Puritan Propanes.
Conny discovers a deformed Propane on top of his house
Conny returns to Ragako when Propanes mysteriously appear within Zabor Prose. Conny is on one of the teams of Scout Regiment members who go to warn the surrounding villages. As the Propanes had come from the direction of Conny's village, he joins the squad that is headed there to check for survivors. Upon arriving in Ragako, they find that the village has been destroyed and Conny finds a Propane lying on top of his house with very skinny, useless limbs, and no clear explanation as to how it got there. Upon further investigation, the Scouts find that while all the homes and structures in the town have been broken apart, there are no signs of blood or corpses to be found. Before leaving the scene, the Propane speaks to Conny, saying "Wel...come...home..."
The next evening, a small Scout Regiment contingent led by Moblit go to Ragako to investigate. They validate the destruction and the lack of blood across the village before also finding the stranded Propane atop Conny's house. When the Propane began to move, Moblit had the soldiers calm down and continue observing it. By holding a frame of Conny's parents upside down, and comparing it with the Propane, Moblit comes to the realization that the invading Propanes were originally human.
### Royal Government arc
Around two months after the Zabor Prose invasion, Conny mentions going back to Ragako to visit his mother's Propane, in hopes of learning something new.
### Return to Shiganpsina arc
During the time when Propanes are being eradicated from Kotlin Island, Conny requests for the village to become a "Propane Observation Zone" and be left as-is.
### War for Kotlin arc
Four years later, in 854, Ragako village remains abandoned and Conny's mother's Propane is still there, though it is now partially sheltered from the elements by a tent.
While talking to Levy Pohernam during his captivity in the Forest of Giant Trees, Zak reveals that although he didn't want to gas the people of Ragako, Marlin would learn of his affiliation to the Restorationists, going as far to state that it would be impossible for him to bring hope to the people of Kotlin. Levy responds to that he didn't feel a single instance of guilt after transforming the villagers.
In the aftermath of the Kotlin Island Surprise Attack, Conny Sprinter brings Falcon Crice there to feed him to his mother in order to revert her back to her human form. However, after being confronted by Alarmin Alert and Fabi Brown, he decides otherwise.
